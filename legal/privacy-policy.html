Word Sort: Privacy Policy

Star Ladder Games · Effective date: October 24, 2026

Word Sort is a word puzzle game for Android made by Star Ladder Games. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The short version: your progress stays on your phone; the free game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google handles the ads and any purchases.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, hints), your Daily Sort results and streak, your word album and your settings are saved only on your phone, in the game's private storage. We don't receive them and there's no account. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Ads

Word Sort is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some levels, and a video ad when you choose to watch one for a free hint or extra Rush time. To show ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, your IP address and approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings.

Buying Remove Ads (or the Starter Pack) turns off the ads between levels. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available, only if you want them.

Purchases

In-app purchases (Remove Ads, the Starter Pack and hint packs) are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google tells the game which items your account owns so they can be delivered and restored on a new phone.

What we collect

We (Star Ladder Games) don't run servers for the game and don't collect your personal information. The game has no analytics of its own. The ad and purchase information above is collected by Google under its own policies.

Sharing your result

When you tap "Share result" after a Daily Sort, the game hands the text you see (your moves as coloured squares, par, stars and streak) to your phone's share sheet. It goes only to the app and people you choose. Word Sort doesn't send it anywhere itself.

Children

Word Sort isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here, with the game's Data safety section on Google Play updated to match, before the version that needs it is released.
